When planning a trip from Delhi to Jaipur, both **train** and **bus** are viable transportation options. Here's a comparison to help you decide which mode of travel suits your needs best:

---

### ✈️ **Train (Railway)**
**Pros:**
- **Reliability:** Trains are generally more punctual and less affected by traffic.
- **Comfort:** Trains offer seating, air conditioning (AC) options, and sometimes even sleeper coaches for overnight journeys.
- **Safety:** Trains are considered safer than buses, especially on long distances.
- **Scenic views:** You can enjoy the changing landscapes of Rajasthan from the window.
- **Connectivity:** Trains connect to multiple cities and can be a good option if you plan to travel further after reaching Jaipur.

**Cons:**
- **Time:** The journey can take around **7–9 hours**, depending on the train.
- **Crowds:** During peak seasons or festivals, trains can get very crowded.
- **Cost:** AC trains can be more expensive than buses, though non-AC options are available.

**Popular Trains:**
- **Delhi–Jaipur Express (14611/14612)**
- **Delhi–Jaipur AC Special (12411/12412)**
- **Delhi–Jaipur Shatabdi Express (12413/12414)**

---

### 🚌 **Bus (Road)**
**Pros:**
- **Cost-effective:** Buses are usually the cheapest option, especially for budget travelers.
- **Flexibility:** You can choose from various private and government-run bus operators.
- **Direct routes:** Some buses offer direct services from Delhi to Jaipur, which can be faster than trains.
- **Convenience:** You can board and alight at different points along the way if needed.

**Cons:**
- **Unpredictable:** Buses can be delayed due to traffic, road conditions, or weather.
- **Comfort:** Long-distance buses may not be as comfortable as trains, especially on overnight trips.
- **Safety:** While generally safe, buses can be more vulnerable to accidents compared to trains.
- **Limited facilities:** Most buses do not offer AC, food, or restrooms on board.

**Popular Bus Operators:**
- **SRS Travels**
- **Sri Krishna Travels**
- **Kalyani Travels**
- **Rajasthan State Road Transport Corporation (RSRTC)**

---

### 📌 Tips for Travelers:
- **Book in advance:** Especially during peak seasons or festivals, it's best to book your tickets well ahead.
- **Check timings:** Consider the time of arrival and departure, especially if you have a tight schedule.
- **Pack essentials:** For buses, bring water, snacks, and a jacket. For trains, bring a travel bag and keep your documents safe.
- **Safety first:** Always choose licensed operators and ensure your vehicle is in good condition.

---

### 📅 Estimated Travel Time:
- **Train:** ~7–9 hours
- **Bus:** ~8–12 hours (depending on traffic and route)

---

### 🧭 Final Recommendation:
- **Choose train** if you prioritize comfort, safety, and reliability.
- **Choose bus** if you're on a budget and looking for a more flexible and direct option.

## 📅 **3-Day Itinerary: Jaipur (From Delhi) – Budget Under ₹20,000**

---

### **Day 1: Arrival & Explore Old City & City Palace**
- **Morning:**
  - Arrive in Jaipur by train (approx. 8:00 AM)
  - Check into a budget hotel (optional)
  - **Walk to the City Palace** – A stunning Mughal-era palace with beautiful gardens and a museum.
- **Afternoon:**
  - **Jantar Mantar (Astronomical Observatory)** – A UNESCO World Heritage Site with unique architectural marvels.
- **Evening:**
  - **Street Food Trail** – Try local delicacies like **dalbharta**, **ghevar**, **kheer**, and **mawa kheer** at local street food stalls.
  - **Pink City Sunset** – Enjoy the view of the pink-hued city as the sun sets.

**Accommodation (Optional):** Check into a budget hotel in the Old City area.

---

### **Day 2: Amber Fort & Jaipur Heritage Loop**
- **Morning:**
  - **Amber Fort** – A 16th-century fort located 11 km from Jaipur. Take a **horse ride** to the fort (approx. ₹150 per person).
  - Explore the fort's architecture, museums, and enjoy the panoramic views.
- **Afternoon:**
  - **Jal Mahal (Water Palace)** – A beautiful palace built on an island in the Man Sagar Lake.
- **Evening:**
  - **Birla Palace** – A grand Indo-Saracenic palace with a beautiful garden and a museum.
  - **Dinner at a local restaurant** – Try **dal makhani**, **biryani**, and **chhole bhature**.

---

### **Day 3: Jaipur Markets & Departure**
- **Morning:**
  - **Johari Bazaar** – Explore the famous market for traditional jewelry, textiles, and handicrafts.
  - **Tripoli Bazaar** – A great place to buy spices, sweets, and local souvenirs.
- **Afternoon:**
  - **Chokdi Bazaar** – A local market for everyday items and street food.
  - **Optional:** Visit **Albert Hall Museum** or **Hawa Mahal** if time permits.
- **Evening:**
  - Departure from Jaipur by train back to Delhi.
